"Footwork, drops, mechanics, progressions and protections."

Not arm talent. Not highlights. Those five things are the entire job of reading an NFL defence, and the video says so: "those details matter as much as arm talent." A head coach listing those five specifically is a far stronger endorsement than any workout clip.
